Understand the process of account creation
1
Enter the Aadhaar number of student
2
Enter the name of the student as in Aadhaar
3
Enter the gender of the student as in Aadhaar
4
Enter the mobile number of the student / parent
5
Enter the e-mail address of the student (not mandatory)
Understand the process of Application Submission (Application is common for all schemes)
1
Enter Class 10 (SSLC / CBSE / ICSE / KOS) Registration Number of the student
2
Enter the RD number of Caste, Income, EWS certificate issued by Revenue Department (whichever is applicable)
3
Enter the Unique Disability ID (UDID) of the student issued by Govt of India (If the student is a person with Disabilities)
4
Enter University/ Educational Board Registration number of the student for the current course
5
Enter the Counselling number, year and course details (if the student is admitted to any professional course through counselling)
6
Enter Class 12 (SSLC / CBSE / ICSE) Registration Number of the student (only for 1st year students studying Undergraduate courses)
7
Enter previous academic year University / Board Registration Number and other details to fetch result details (For students other than SC and ST category)
8
Student must upload documents for e-attestation from concerned department officer (if applicable) (Check out the list for applicability to upload documents and concerned approving authority)
9
Select whether the student is a Dayscholar or a Hosteller
10
Enter One Time Registration (OTR) number issued by National Scholarship Portal (only for SC, ST and OBC students)

Enter One Time Registration (OTR) number issued by National Scholarship Portal (only for SC, ST and OBC students)
2
Enter the SATS ID given by the school
3
Enter the RD number of Caste, Income, EWS certificate issued by Revenue Department (whichever is applicable)

6
Upload Unclean Occupation Certificate issued by Competent Authority (if applicable) (An unclean occupation certificate is an official document issued by a designated authority to certify that a parent or guardian is engaged in occupations considered hazardous or stigmatized, such as manual scavenging, waste collection, tanning, or flaying, for the purpose of availing pre-matric scholarships.)

About Us

The State Scholarship Portal (SSP) – a unified platform developed by Center for e-Governance, DPAR (e-Governance), Government of Karnataka ensures disbursement of Prematric & Postmatric Scholarship under various schemes of Govt of Karnataka. SSP provides a platform for receipt of online applications and disbursal of scholarship by sponsoring departments through DBT to the Aadhar seeded bank account of the beneficiary.
