## Upstream sources

The records are compiled from public government sources: the Karnataka Public
Procurement Portal, NIC e-procurement (GePNIC) portals, and BBMP work-order and
payment data republished by OpenCity (public domain). Ward boundaries come from
OpenCity (public domain). Each record names its source and links to it.
Contractors' phone numbers present in some upstream files are removed.
